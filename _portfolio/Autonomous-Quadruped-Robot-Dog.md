Designed and simulated an autonomous 12-DoF quadruped robot to explore adaptive locomotion across challenging terrains, including mud and swampy environments. Modeled in Autodesk Fusion 360 and exported as a custom URDF with precise inertial parameters, the robot was integrated with ROS 2, Gazebo, and the CHAMP quadruped framework for dynamic balance control and simulation.

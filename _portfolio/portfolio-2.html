--- title: "Handwritten Amharic Characters Dataset" collection: portfolio type: "Research Project & Dataset" permalink: /portfolio/amharic-character-dataset venue: "IEEE" date: 2019-09-19 excerpt: "![Handwritten Amharic Character Samples] (/images/handWritten dataset.png)" --- As part of my published IEEE research on handwritten Amharic character recognition using CNNs, I constructed a comprehensive dataset featuring 30,446 total handwritten samples (27,413 training and 3,033 testing) across 286 distinct character classes. This dataset is currently being prepared for public release on Kaggle. [Dataset Coming Soon on Kaggle] (https://kaggle.com)

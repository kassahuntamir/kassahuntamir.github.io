excerpt: 'Amharic alphabet has about 34 families of characters and about eight members with in a family, with some exceptions. There are, a total of, 286 different characters (286 classes). For this research, I have collected 30,446 characters from about 130 different individuals. Among them 27,413 are used for training and the remaining 3,033 are used for testing. Convolutional Neural Network algorithm with two convolutional layers is employed to classify the aforementioned characters to 286 classes. The design is evaluated on Keras and TensorFlow frameworks. After training the system, I have got a training accuracy around 99.52% after the first epoch, which is the least of accuracies of other epochs and a testing accuracy of 99.71%, approximately.'
